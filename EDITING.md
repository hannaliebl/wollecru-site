# How to edit the Wollecru website

You don't need any technical knowledge or a GitHub account.

## Getting in

1. Look for an email invitation from **Pages CMS**. Click the link.
2. Bookmark **https://app.pagescms.org**. That's where you'll edit from now on.
3. Choose the **wollecru-site** project.

## What you can edit

| In the menu | What it changes |
|-------------|-----------------|
| **Site settings** | The name, pronunciation and tagline at the top, the "Shop" link, the contact email, the logo |
| **Intro (Farming Healthy Soil)** | The first section of text |
| **Wool / Flora / Wine section** | Each section is a list of parts: a heading, some text, then photos. Drag parts or photos to reorder them. |
| **Contact and newsletter section** | Address, Instagram, newsletter text, the photo at the bottom, photo credits |

## Saving

Click **Save**. The live website updates in about **one minute**. There's no separate "publish" step, so what you save is public.

Made a mistake? Fix it and save again. Every change is recorded, so any change can be undone.

## Tips

- **Photos:** Landscape (wide) photos at least 1600 pixels wide look best. Fill in the **photo description**. It's read aloud to people using screen readers.
- **Links:** Always paste the full address, starting with `https://`.
- **The big photos behind the section titles** (wool, flora, wine, contact) can't be changed here yet. Ask Hanna.
